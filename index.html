Scoring Mechanism

Event-Adjusted

One conviction score compares the information shock with expectations, valuation, and the market's first adjustment. It is a research rank, not a return probability.

Three-Layer Surprise

Numerical: earnings, revenue, margins, cash flow, and guidance. Narrative: demand acceleration, pricing power, supply constraints, and management confidence. Expectation: whether the new evidence is genuinely better than what valuation and consensus already imply.

Reaction-Gap Test

Strong fundamentals are not enough. The model asks whether the stock and its abnormal return versus SOXX have already absorbed the information. Positive evidence plus a weak or orderly price response can improve entry quality; a euphoric gap or parabolic trend reduces it.

Risk And Thesis Overlay

The score ranks ideas; it does not set position size. Initial exposure is reduced for earnings events, volatility, correlated semiconductor holdings, customer concentration, and crowding. A broken guidance, demand, margin, or supply-chain thesis forces review even if price has not hit a stop.
