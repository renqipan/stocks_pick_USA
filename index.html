Investment Interpretation

The capital-flow signal favors companies with pricing power at choke points: NVIDIA in accelerated compute; Broadcom, Marvell, and Credo in custom silicon and networking; Micron in HBM; TSMC, Lam Research, AMAT, KLA, FormFactor, and Camtek in capacity, packaging, inspection, and test; Coherent and Lumentum in optics; and Vertiv and Eaton in grid-to-rack infrastructure. Microsoft proves AI infrastructure can monetize quickly, while Alphabet and Meta show that free cash flow can still be squeezed. The chain remains strong, but capital efficiency and entry price now matter more.

AI Data Center Supply Chain

The constraint is moving from chips alone to financed, powered, and networked AI factories. Through 2028, the key pressure points are HBM allocation, CoWoS-class packaging, process control, 800G/1.6T data movement, storage access, rack power, liquid cooling, and shovel-ready sites.

Bottleneck Migration

Silicon -> System -> Site

NVIDIA's direct site investments and third-party financing platforms show that deployment speed now depends on capital, land, grid access, cooling, and serviceability alongside accelerator supply.

Memory Constraint

HBM Specs Bend

TrendForce expects 2027 HBM bit growth of 50%-60% to remain below demand. NVIDIA and CSPs are evaluating lower-memory configurations because wafer allocation and HBM4e validation cannot be assumed.

Connectivity Constraint

Data Movement Wins

Rubin racks require 800G networking, DPUs, liquid cooling, and fast storage access. CPO, silicon photonics, AECs, and GPU-initiated storage increasingly determine useful AI output per megawatt.

Scoring Mechanism

Event-Adjusted

One conviction score compares the information shock with expectations, valuation, and the market's first adjustment. It is a research rank, not a return probability.

Three-Layer Surprise

Numerical: earnings, revenue, margins, cash flow, and guidance. Narrative: demand acceleration, pricing power, supply constraints, and management confidence. Expectation: whether the new evidence is genuinely better than what valuation and consensus already imply.

Reaction-Gap Test

Strong fundamentals are not enough. The model asks whether the stock and its abnormal return versus SOXX have already absorbed the information. Positive evidence plus a weak or orderly price response can improve entry quality; a euphoric gap or parabolic trend reduces it.

Risk And Thesis Overlay

The score ranks ideas; it does not set position size. Initial exposure is reduced for earnings events, volatility, correlated semiconductor holdings, customer concentration, and crowding. A broken guidance, demand, margin, or supply-chain thesis forces review even if price has not hit a stop.
