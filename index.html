Selected Research Candidates

PDFSPlatform

PDF Solutions | $30.44 Nov. 20 close

Why investigate: Chiplets, 3D integration and hybrid packaging increase cross-fab data complexity. Exensio Aurora could make yield analytics, equipment connectivity and workflow automation more valuable.

Reported evidence: November 5 Q3 release: revenue $68.3M, +20% YoY; GAAP net income $6.841M and EPS $0.16. Backlog fell to $247.2M from $270.7M. New Q3 periodic filing was not independently reviewed.

Price discipline: At $30.44, annualizing Q3 GAAP EPS $0.16 produces about 47.6x, not a consensus forward multiple. Backlog contraction and uncertain recurring-software economics prevent a fresh buy.

What can break: Customer in-house tools or larger software competitors win; platform growth stalls; hardware mix, integration costs or dilution prevent a 20% net margin.

AEHRAdoption

Aehr Test Systems | $39.84 Nov. 20 close

Why investigate: Higher-power AI packages and photonic devices increase the cost of latent failures. Wafer/package-level burn-in and device-specific consumables could capture more test content.

Reported evidence: November 2 FY27 Q1 release: revenue $33.007M, +200% YoY; GAAP net income $7.181M and EPS $0.21. Operating cash flow was -$3.054M. FY27 ~$150M revenue and at least 20% non-GAAP net margin are management guidance, not realized GAAP results.

Price discipline: At $39.84, Q1 EPS annualization gives about 47.4x. Positive profit with negative operating cash flow, concentrated orders and dilution need scrutiny. Orders and pilot systems do not guarantee accepted volume shipments.

What can break: Benchmark wins do not convert, major customers delay capex, burn-in methods change, or concentration and dilution overwhelm revenue growth.

VICRPower IP

Vicor | $125.09 Nov. 20 close

Why investigate: Denser accelerators need efficient high-current delivery close to the processor. Vertical power delivery and compact modules could gain content as rack power distribution evolves.

Reported evidence: Q3 revenue $197.114M comprises $130.501M product revenue and $66.613M royalties; attributable GAAP profit $63.321M. The October 26 10-Q is available through the issuer.

Price discipline: At $125.09, annualizing Q3 EPS $1.33 gives about 23.5x. Royalties were $66.613M of $197.114M revenue; licensing timing makes that a poor proxy for normalized recurring earnings.

What can break: TI or other architectures win, patent/licensing economics disappoint, customers redesign power delivery, or manufacturing expansion consumes returns.
