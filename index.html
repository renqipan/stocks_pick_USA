Investment Interpretation

The capital-flow signal favors companies with pricing power at choke points: NVIDIA in accelerated compute; Broadcom, Marvell, and Credo in custom silicon and networking; Micron in HBM; TSMC, AMAT, KLA, FormFactor, and Camtek in capacity, packaging, inspection, and test; Coherent and Lumentum in optics; and Vertiv and Eaton in grid-to-rack infrastructure. The thesis is intact, but the July 14 pre-market selloff makes stabilization and position sizing more important than catching the first bounce.

FormFactor Earnings Date High

FormFactor will report Q2 2026 results on July 29. The key test is whether revenue reaches the $240M +/- $5M guide and GAAP EPS reaches $0.46 +/- $0.04 while HBM and networking probe-card demand remains strong.

TrendForce High

HBM pricing power, 3nm/2nm and 2.5D/3D packaging constraints, 1.6T optics, and early 800V HVDC adoption all point to a bottleneck stack that now reaches from wafers to the facility.

SIA / Deloitte High

The June AI data center semiconductor report says semiconductors account for more than 95% of AI server-rack content value, more than 50% of AI data center build-and-operate capex, and could reach US$1.2T of annual AI data center semiconductor revenue by 2028.

EE Times High

May 28 coverage highlights memory pooling AI server architecture with up to 100 TB DRAM per accelerator and near-packaged optical interconnects for next-generation data centers.

SemiAnalysis High

The latest accessible deep-dive frames 2026 as a silicon-shortage year: TSMC N3, memory, packaging, and data center power all constrain how fast hyperscalers can deploy AI compute.

TechInsights High

Its 2026 AI Outlook keeps the long-term map centered on GPUs, ASICs, next-generation memory, 2nm GAA, co-packaged optics, and datacenter compute surpassing $600B by 2030.

SEMI High

SEMI's Q2 2026 300mm outlook projects record US$142B front-end equipment spending in 2026, +25% YoY, with Logic & Micro still the largest segment and Memory spending projected to grow sharply.

NVIDIA / Optics High

NVIDIA says Vera Rubin is ramping into full production, and TrendForce's CPO/NPO work says CPO adoption can accelerate with Rubin Ultra by late 2027. This keeps optical engines, lasers, DSPs, retimers, and silicon photonics central to the rack thesis.

Micron Earnings High

Micron's fiscal Q3 release confirms the memory supercycle: revenue reached $41.46B, GAAP net income was $28.24B, operating cash flow was $25.39B, and Q4 revenue guidance is $50.0B +/- $1.0B. MU moves from event-risk caution to post-earnings leader that needs a calmer base.

IEA / Grid Constraint High

IEA expects data centers to drive about half of US electricity-demand growth through 2030 and says more than 2,500 GW of generation, storage, and large-load projects are stalled in grid-connection queues worldwide.

Schneider Electric Medium

Its AI factory infrastructure note frames power and liquid cooling as a unified grid-to-chip and chip-to-chiller architecture. For this dashboard, that shifts the stock screen toward power-aware semis and away from pure accelerator-only enthusiasm.

AI Data Center Supply Chain

The constraint is moving from chips alone to the full AI factory. Through 2028, the key pressure points are advanced packaging and test, HBM, 1.6T connectivity, and grid-to-rack power plus liquid cooling.

Bottleneck Migration

Chip -> Rack -> Site

NVIDIA's Vera Rubin ramp, GB300 rack evidence, and TrendForce's 800V rack-power work all point to the same direction: deployment speed now depends on packaging, rack power, cooling, networking, and serviceability as much as accelerator supply.

Memory Constraint

HBM + LPDRAM

Micron's record fiscal Q3 and TrendForce's late-June HBM report list confirm that memory pricing, certification, and allocation remain central to the AI data center bottleneck.

Connectivity Constraint

1.6T+

TrendForce's CPO/NPO work adds another path for 1.6T+ bandwidth: optical engines, laser sources, DSPs, retimers, and silicon photonics stay essential, but concentration risk remains high.
