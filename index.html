PDF Solutions | $40.82 Oct. 26 close

Why investigate: Chiplets, 3D integration and hybrid packaging increase cross-fab data complexity. Exensio Aurora could make yield analytics, equipment connectivity and workflow automation more valuable.

Reported evidence: Q2 revenue $61.5M, +19% YoY; GAAP net income $4.271M; platform revenue $49.1M. The filing says Exensio/software and secureWISE decreases partly offset hardware-led growth.

Price discipline: $40.82 / ($0.10 quarterly GAAP EPS x 4) is about 102x. Small size does not mean a cheap stock or a SaaS-like margin structure.

What can break: Customer in-house tools or larger software competitors win; platform growth stalls; hardware mix, integration costs or dilution prevent a 20% net margin.

AEHRAdoption

Aehr Test Systems | $56.60 Oct. 26 close

Why investigate: Higher-power AI packages and photonic devices increase the cost of latent failures. Wafer/package-level burn-in and device-specific consumables could capture more test content.

Reported evidence: FY26 revenue $50.0M and GAAP loss $7.126M; $3.310M operating cash outflow. FY27 management revenue outlook is $130M-$150M, not reported sales. October 7 follow-on Sonoma orders were about $6M.

Price discipline: The business is priced for an inflection. FY26 cash growth was largely equity-financed; 32.62M shares were outstanding July 20. The 50M terminal assumption explicitly allows substantial dilution. The modeled 25% terminal net margin is more demanding than management's FY27 18%-22% non-GAAP net-income outlook.

What can break: Benchmark wins do not convert, major customers delay capex, burn-in methods change, or concentration and dilution overwhelm revenue growth.
